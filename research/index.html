Research / Jin Hu

Understanding failures.
Developing reliable AI.

My research advances trustworthy AI through generative evaluation and adaptive optimization. I study how visual and multimodal models respond to changing scenes, semantic constraints, and physical conditions.

A shared question

How can we discover meaningful model failures while controlling the conditions that produce them? My work connects the generation of evaluation examples with the feedback used to optimize them.

Two first-author papers establish complementary foundations: DynamicPAE improves feedback during generator training, while InSUR improves guidance during diffusion inference. Their collaborative extensions explore physical perception and consistent observations across sensing modalities.

01 / Learning from noisy feedback

DynamicPAE

IEEE TPAMI 2026 · First author

Generating Scene-Aware Physical Adversarial Examples in Real-Time

Paper ↗Code ↗

Physical transformations introduce noisy feedback when training an adversarial generator. DynamicPAE learns to generate an example conditioned on the observed scene, making scene adaptation possible through a single forward pass.

Residual-guided training
An auxiliary reconstruction task supplies richer feedback and helps the generator explore useful scene-dependent patterns.
Adaptive objective control
A controller based on loss-distribution skewness adjusts competing objectives, while conditional scene modeling accounts for incomplete observations and physical variation.

This work establishes a foundation in adaptive optimization for physical reliability evaluation.

02 / Controlling generative inference

InSUR

NeurIPS 2025 · First author

Exploring Semantic-constrained Adversarial Example with Instruction Uncertainty Reduction

Paper ↗Project ↗Code ↗

Language-guided evaluation requires generated examples to preserve the intended semantics while exposing recognition failures. The InSUR framework connects diffusion guidance, scene constraints, and semantic evaluation.

Residual approximation for guidance
ResAdv-DDIM uses a short prediction of the future denoising outcome to stabilize current guidance during diffusion sampling.
Semantic and scene constraints
Spatial guidance and differentiable rendering extend generation to 2D images and 3D objects. Abstracted labels and paired examples relate evaluation to the intended semantics.

This work connects controllable visual generation with evaluation of visual and vision-language models.
